# Employee Cost Calculator

An employee cost calculator adds everything an employer pays on top of wages. This tool applies the 2026 US federal employer payroll taxes, your state unemployment rate, benefits and overhead to a salary or hourly wage. It returns the total yearly cost, the loaded hourly cost and the multiplier over base pay.

## How to Use the Employee Cost Calculator

Choose Salaried or Hourly, enter pay, then fill in the taxes and benefits you know, and select Calculate.

- **Salaried** uses an annual salary and paid hours per year.
- **Hourly** uses an hourly wage, hours per week and paid weeks per year.

State unemployment, benefits and overhead are optional. Load example fills in an $85,000 hire with typical benefits.

## Employee Cost Formulas

| Cost | 2026 rule |
|---|---|
| Social Security (employer) | 6.2% of wages up to $184,500 |
| Medicare (employer) | 1.45% of all wages |
| FUTA | 0.6% of the first $7,000, after the standard credit |
| State unemployment | Your rate × wages up to your state's wage base |
| Retirement match | Match % × wages |
| Total cost | Wages + taxes + benefits + overhead |

The Social Security rate and wage base come from the [SSA's 2026 figures](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/cbb.html). FUTA rules follow [IRS Topic 759](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc759), which explains the 6% gross rate and the 5.4% credit for paying state unemployment tax on time.

## Worked Example

A company hires at $85,000. Employer Social Security is $5,270 and Medicare is $1,232.50. FUTA adds $42, and state unemployment at 2.7% of a $7,000 base adds $189.

Benefits include a $650 monthly health premium ($7,800 a year), a 4% retirement match ($3,400) and $600 of other benefits. Overhead is $3,000. The total is about $106,534, or 1.25 times salary. Over 2,080 hours, that is about $51.22 an hour.

## Why the Loaded Cost Matters

Budgeting on salary alone understates hiring costs, often by a fifth or more. The loaded hourly figure is also the floor for pricing client work, which the [billable hours calculator](/tools/billable-hours-calculator/) builds on. Companies weighing staff against software can compare the result with the savings estimated in the [ROI calculator](/tools/roi-calculator/).

## Methodology and Limitations

- Federal taxes use 2026 rates. The Additional Medicare Tax is withheld from the employee and is not an employer cost.
- FUTA assumes the full 5.4% state credit. Employers in credit-reduction states pay more.
- State unemployment rates and wage bases vary by state and employer history, so enter your own.
- Local taxes, paid leave mandates and bonuses are not modeled unless you add them as other benefits.
- Inputs stay in your browser. A calculation is saved locally after you select Calculate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does an Employee Cost Beyond Salary?

It varies with benefits, but payroll taxes alone add about 7.65% for most wages. Health insurance, retirement matching and equipment often push the total to 1.25 to 1.4 times salary.

### What Payroll Taxes Does an Employer Pay in 2026?

Employers pay 6.2% Social Security on wages up to $184,500, 1.45% Medicare on all wages, federal unemployment tax on the first $7,000, and state unemployment tax at their assigned rate.

### What Is a Loaded Labor Rate?

A loaded labor rate is the total employment cost divided by paid hours. It includes wages, taxes, benefits and overhead, so it shows what one hour of an employee's time really costs.

### Does the Calculator Include State Income Tax?

No. State income tax is withheld from the employee's pay, so it is not an employer cost. State unemployment tax is an employer cost, and you can enter your rate.
