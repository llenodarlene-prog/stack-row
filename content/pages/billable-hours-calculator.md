# Billable Hours Calculator

A billable hours calculator turns your working schedule into the hours you can actually invoice. This tool applies a utilization rate to your weekly hours and working weeks, then shows annual billings at your rate. A second tab works backward from a target income to the minimum hourly rate you need.

## How to Use the Billable Hours Calculator

Enter hours worked per week, working weeks per year and your utilization rate. Then choose a tab and select Calculate.

- **Billable Hours** shows billable and non-billable hours, plus annual billings if you add a rate.
- **Required Rate** uses a target take-home income, business expenses and a tax set-aside to find your minimum rate.

Load example fills in a consultant working 46 weeks at 70% utilization.

## Billable Hours Formulas

| Result | Formula |
|---|---|
| Available hours | Hours per week × working weeks |
| Billable hours | Available hours × utilization |
| Annual billings | Billable hours × hourly rate |
| Revenue needed | (Income + expenses) ÷ (1 − tax rate) |
| Minimum hourly rate | Revenue needed ÷ billable hours |

Utilization is the share of working time spent on client work. The rest goes to sales, admin, training and email. The [IRS self-employment tax guide](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes) explains the 15.3% tax that sits on top of income tax for freelancers.

## Worked Example

A consultant works 40 hours a week for 46 weeks, which is 1,840 hours. At 70% utilization, 1,288 of those hours are billable.

They want $95,000 take-home, expect $12,000 of business costs and set aside 25% for taxes. Revenue needed is ($95,000 + $12,000) ÷ 0.75 = $142,667. The minimum rate is $142,667 ÷ 1,288 = about $110.77 an hour.

## Why Utilization Changes Everything

Few people bill every hour they work. Moving from 70% to 60% utilization in the example raises the minimum rate to about $129 an hour. ## Methodology and Limitations

- The tax set-aside is a single blended rate you choose. Actual taxes depend on your location and deductions.
- Utilization applies evenly across the year. Seasonal work may need a lower average.
- The required rate is a floor with no profit margin or buffer for unpaid invoices.
- Currency selection changes the displayed symbol only. Inputs stay in your browser.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Many Billable Hours Are in a Year?

At 40 hours a week for 48 weeks, a full year is 1,920 working hours. At a 75% utilization rate, that is 1,440 billable hours. Your number depends on time off and admin load.

### What Is a Good Utilization Rate?

It depends on the role. Many consulting and agency teams target 70% to 85% for delivery staff, while owners who also sell and manage usually bill a lower share of their time.

### How Do I Calculate My Hourly Rate?

Add your target income and business expenses, divide by one minus your tax rate, then divide by your billable hours. That gives the minimum rate before any profit margin.

### Should I Bill for Admin Time?

Usually not directly. Build admin into your rate by using a realistic utilization figure, so the hours you do bill cover the time you cannot.
