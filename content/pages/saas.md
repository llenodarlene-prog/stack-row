# SaaS Insights Beyond the Subscription Price

Software subscriptions connect a commercial promise to ongoing work. Stack Row’s SaaS coverage examines adoption, revenue, pricing, and the operating questions behind buying and keeping business software.

## Read the Market With the Right Measures {backdrop:coastal-workspace}

Supplier revenue can describe the size of a business. Adoption research can describe use within a surveyed population. Neither establishes which applications your organization needs or how many seats it should buy.

Our focus is the relationship between the market evidence and the subscription decision. That includes the meaning of a growth figure, the scope of a pricing unit, and the work involved in changing an existing software arrangement.
