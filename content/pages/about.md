# About Stack Row

Stack Row is an independent publication about the technology systems that businesses buy, operate, secure, and scale.

## Editorial purpose

Technology decisions often rely on figures that use different populations, time periods, and definitions. Stack Row traces claims to their sources, explains those differences, and avoids treating vendor material as independent proof.

## How the work is produced

Every published article begins with a research record. Sources, statistics, claims, search intent, competitor coverage, internal links, and QA sign-offs are documented before publication.

Stack Row does not publish invented data, undisclosed personal experience, or unverified product claims.
