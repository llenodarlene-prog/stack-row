# Contact Stack Row

Have a question about our coverage, a correction to suggest, or a technology topic worth examining? Email [stackrow@gmail.com](mailto:stackrow@gmail.com).

A clear subject line and relevant details help us understand your message.

## What to Include

You can use the same email address for each of the following inquiries.

::: cards
### Corrections and Source Updates {icon:edit}
Include the page URL, the statement or figure concerned, and a source supporting the change. If a product document or report has been updated, point us to the relevant version. Suggested subject: `Correction: [Page Title]`

### Research Leads and Topic Suggestions {icon:lightbulb}
Tell us the question you think needs answering and why it matters to people working with business technology. Share original research or official documentation where possible. Suggested subject: `Research Lead: [Topic]`

### Contributor Proposals {icon:users}
Send a short outline, the evidence you plan to use, and relevant writing samples. Disclose any employment, client, sponsorship, or other commercial relationship connected to the subject. Suggested subject: `Contributor Proposal: [Topic]`

### Business Inquiries and Permissions {icon:building}
Explain the purpose of your inquiry and name the organization you represent. For reuse requests, include the content URL, the material you want to use, and where it would appear. Suggested subject: `Business Inquiry: [Organization or Request]`
:::

## Send Only What We Need

Please do not email passwords, access keys, confidential customer records, or other sensitive information. A public source and a clear description are usually enough to begin an editorial conversation.

For privacy questions, use the subject “Privacy Request.” Details about the handling of personal information belong in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

:::
