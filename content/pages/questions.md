# Technology Questions and Answers

Stack Row's answer center groups common technology questions by topic. Each answer opens with a short reply that stands on its own. Examples, tables, and steps follow where they help.

## How These Answers Are Written

Every answer here follows the same rules, so you know what you're getting.

- **Answer first.** The opening lines answer the question without needing the rest of the page.
- **Sources you can check.** Facts link to primary sources like NIST, vendor documentation, or open-source project docs.
- **Reviewed regularly.** Each topic page shows the date it was last checked.
- **Linked to the tools.** Where a calculator helps, the answer links straight to it.
