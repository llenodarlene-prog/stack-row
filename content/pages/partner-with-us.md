# Partner With Stack Row

Reach the people who choose, build, and run business technology. Stack Row works with technology and SaaS companies on sponsored posts, guest posts, and brand features that meet the same evidence standard as the rest of the publication.

::: actions

## Ways to Work With Us {#options}

Choose the format that fits what you want readers to understand. Every option is reviewed by our editors before it is published.

::: cards
### Sponsored Posts {icon:megaphone}
A dedicated piece about your product, research, or point of view, published on Stack Row and labeled as sponsored content.

### Guest Posts {icon:edit}
An article written by a practitioner on your team and published under their name. It should teach readers something useful about a technology decision.

### Brand Features {icon:star}
A focused look at a product, launch, or company, based on public documentation and the material you supply.

### Custom Partnerships {icon:users}
Have a different idea, such as a content series or a research collaboration? Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you whether it fits.
:::

## Our Standards for Partner Content

Partner content appears next to our own research, so it follows the same rules. These standards protect readers and the brands that work with us.

::: cards
### Clear Disclosure {icon:tag}
Paid and partner content is labeled so readers can tell it apart from independent editorial coverage.

### Evidence Behind Every Claim {icon:document}
Statistics, pricing, and product claims need a source readers can inspect. We do not publish invented figures or unverifiable comparisons.

### Editorial Review {icon:search}
Our editors review every submission. We may ask for changes, and we may decline content that does not suit our readers.

### No Guaranteed Outcomes {icon:check}
We do not promise search rankings, traffic, or sales. A partnership is a commitment to publish agreed content, not a performance guarantee.
:::

## What to Send

- The format you are interested in
- The topic or product you want to cover, and the reader you want to reach
- Your preferred timing
- Any existing draft, outline, or source material

Rates and availability are shared in reply to your inquiry.
