# Technology decisions, examined with evidence

Stack Row organizes technical and commercial evidence for the people responsible for business technology. The publication separates comparable measures, documents source limits, and turns fragmented research into useful operating context.

## Research for technology operators

Coverage focuses on the decisions behind secure systems, software portfolios, AI deployment, infrastructure economics, and enterprise purchasing.

## Built around definitions, not headlines

A security incident is not automatically a breach. AI investment is not production use. Cloud spending is not the same measure as deployed workloads. Stack Row keeps those distinctions visible.
