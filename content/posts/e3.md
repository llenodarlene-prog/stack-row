# CRM Software for Small Companies: 7 Options, Costs and Best Fits

CRM software for small companies should make customer records, ownership, and next steps easy to find. Start with the workflow your team repeats daily, then compare the plans that support it.

HubSpot offers a limited free starting point. Zoho CRM supports small teams seeking configurable workflows. Pipedrive focuses on sales pipelines, while Freshsales combines sales records with communication features.

Capsule, Less Annoying CRM, and Salesforce Starter Suite provide other useful starting points. Their pricing structures and upgrade paths differ enough to change the buying decision.

These seven options serve different needs. The comparison uses official product information checked on October 25, 2026. It does not claim hands-on testing or a universal winner.

## Why Shared Customer Data Deserves Attention

A CRM purchase often begins with missed follow-ups or customer information scattered across several applications. Adding another application only helps when the team agrees where records belong.

Salesforce surveyed 3,350 SMB leaders across 26 countries for its sixth Small & Medium Business Trends report. Respondents represented businesses with 200 or fewer employees. Fieldwork ran from August 3 through September 16, 2024.

In that vendor-sponsored survey, [53% reported inconsistent data across business tools](https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/small-business/smb-trends-report-6th-edition.pdf). Another 46% felt overwhelmed by too many tools. These figures describe surveyed global SMB leaders, not US CRM adoption rates.

The useful question is practical: will the new system reduce duplicate work? A purchase alone cannot establish reliable ownership, clean records, or consistent follow-up habits.

## How to Compare the Shortlist

Choose a CRM against a small set of required jobs. A long feature checklist can hide the work your team needs most.

Start with these questions:

- Can each lead have a named owner and next action?
- Can your team capture the emails it needs without excessive manual copying?
- Can managers review stalled deals and overdue tasks?
- Can you export records in a usable format?
- Which required features need a higher subscription tier?

Security and account administration belong in the same discussion. Decide who can export contacts, change pipelines, and remove users. Confirm those controls in the specific plan you intend to buy.

Most products here follow a [business-to-business SaaS model](/saas/what-is-b2b-saas/). Recurring subscriptions make renewal terms and growing seat counts part of the purchase decision. Wider [B2B technology buying research](/business-technology/b2b-tech-statistics/) explains why capability and the quoted tier need separate checks.

## Which CRM Fits Your Workflow?

Start with two candidates that match your daily work. Compare the exact subscription tiers before comparing the vendors' broad platform descriptions.

### 1. HubSpot: A Limited Free Starting Point

HubSpot combines contact, deal, and task management with tools such as meeting scheduling and email tracking. Its [free CRM supports two users and 1,000 contacts](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm).

That limit gives a small team a defined starting point. It does not support a three-person deployment entirely within the free user allowance.

During evaluation, list the features that need paid access. Separate sales requirements from marketing and service requirements before requesting a price.

HubSpot deserves consideration when several customer-facing functions need shared records. Verify the complete subscription combination before treating the free edition as your long-term budget.

### 2. Zoho CRM: Configurable Sales Processes

Zoho CRM offers a free edition for three users and several paid editions. Its current Standard description includes multiple pipelines, email integration, custom modules, and sales forecasting.

Zoho also distinguishes full organization users from more restricted team users. These license types provide different access rights. Do not assume a cheaper supporting-user license replaces a salesperson's license.

Shortlist Zoho when your company already uses related Zoho products or needs configurable customer workflows. During the trial, build one real pipeline and test the reporting your manager expects.

Confirm current US pricing directly before purchasing. The retrieved pricing table did not consistently expose its currency-dependent amounts.

### 3. Pipedrive: Pipeline-Focused Selling

Pipedrive's current tiers are Lite, Growth, Premium, and Ultimate. Its entry-level offering covers leads, deals, contacts, calendar activity, and pipeline management.

The product provides a useful evaluation starting point for teams organizing sales around deal stages. Growth adds an emphasis on automated emails and follow-ups.

List the communication features your representatives need before choosing a tier. Check email synchronization, automation allowances, and any paid add-ons in the current comparison.

The pricing page retrieved during research displayed euros. Avoid treating that regional display as a US-dollar quote. Request the US total for your intended seat count and billing term.

### 4. Freshsales: Communication Alongside Sales Records

Freshsales Growth lists chat, email, phone, custom fields, and basic workflows. Its advertised annual-billing rate is $9 per user monthly.

Pro lists additional capabilities such as contact scoring, territory management, and sales sequences. Enterprise lists field-level permissions, custom modules, a sandbox, and audit logs.

The [Freshsales plan comparison separates these features and optional add-ons](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/). This distinction helps explain why a low entry price may not match your final requirements.

Check communication charges, document requirements, and any AI usage before budgeting. Evaluate the tier your business needs, rather than assuming Growth includes every advertised capability.

### 5. Capsule: Contacts and Opportunities

Capsule's free plan supports two users and 250 contacts. Its entry paid tier advertises $18 per user monthly, billed annually, with a much larger contact allowance. Capsule has renamed tiers recently, so confirm the current plan name.

Its product structure includes contact management, sales pipelines, projects, and tasks. That combination makes it worth evaluating for relationship-led businesses with recurring follow-up work.

Check which automation features your chosen tier includes. A larger contact allowance does not automatically provide every workflow capability.

Build a sample customer record with notes, tasks, and an opportunity during the trial. Ask the person handling daily follow-ups to judge the process.

### 6. Less Annoying CRM: Straightforward Subscription Budgeting

Less Annoying CRM publishes a $15 monthly price per user. Its pricing documentation includes phone and email support and a 30-day trial.

Its product documentation covers contacts, companies, calendars, tasks, pipelines, and custom fields. Those functions provide a focused shortlist option for teams organizing customer work.

A simple price does not remove the need to test requirements. Bring your actual reporting questions and a representative customer record to the trial.

Choose it when the documented workflow fits your business. Avoid assuming that a larger platform's specialized functions exist here without checking them.

### 7. Salesforce Starter Suite: A Combined Starting Point

Salesforce lists Starter Suite at $25 per user monthly, with monthly or annual billing. Its small-business offering combines customer-management functions across sales, marketing, and service.

The pricing page lists Pro Suite separately at $100 per user monthly, billed annually. Requirements around customization, automation, and application access can change which edition fits.

Companies comparing [HubSpot and Salesforce](/business-technology/hubspot-vs-salesforce/) should separate entry-level plans from broader platform capabilities. Features associated with the vendor may require a different edition.

Request a demonstration using your intended workflow. Confirm that the demonstration uses the same subscription your quote includes.

## What Would Three or Ten Users Cost?

Seat arithmetic provides a starting point for budgeting. It does not create a like-for-like feature comparison.

The following examples multiply verified published rates by the stated number of paid users. They exclude taxes, optional services, implementation, and usage charges.

| Selected plan | Three users: monthly equivalent | Ten users: monthly equivalent | Ten users: base annual cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Freshsales Growth, annual billing | $27 | $90 | $1,080 |
| Capsule entry paid tier, annual billing | $54 | $180 | $2,160 |
| Less Annoying CRM, monthly rate | $45 | $150 | $1,800 over 12 months |
| Salesforce Starter Suite | $75 | $250 | $3,000 over 12 months |

For annual subscriptions, a monthly equivalent does not mean the vendor invoices monthly. Confirm the payment schedule before committing.

HubSpot's two-user free allowance cannot cover either paid-team scenario automatically. Zoho's three-user free edition may fit a small team only if its features meet requirements.

For a fuller budget, record these additional items:

- Data cleanup and import work.
- Staff training and administrator time.
- Integrations or external automation subscriptions.
- Extra communication, storage, document, or AI usage.
- The cost of the next required subscription tier.

Assign an owner to each budget assumption. A requirement discovered after purchase can cost more than a modest seat-price difference.

## Run a Practical CRM Trial Before Buying

A useful trial follows one customer through your real process. Keep the exercise small enough that staff can finish it.

Prepare a limited sample containing clean records and a few deliberate duplicates. Include an active deal, a stalled opportunity, and an existing customer.

Then complete these tasks:

1. Import the records and review duplicate handling.
2. Assign owners and schedule follow-up actions.
3. Record an email or meeting using your intended workflow.
4. Move an opportunity through the pipeline.
5. Produce the weekly report your manager needs.
6. Export the records and inspect the result.

Ask users where they needed help. Record the time spent fixing imports and locating information. These observations provide your own evidence about implementation effort.

Also test account removal and reassignment. Customer ownership should remain clear when a representative changes roles or leaves.

## Questions About Small-Company CRM Software


### Can a Small Company Use a Free CRM?

Yes, when its user allowance and features match the company's requirements. HubSpot allows two free users, while Zoho CRM's free edition supports three.

### Is the Cheapest CRM the Lowest-Cost Option?

Not necessarily, because required upgrades, integrations, and administration can increase total spending. Compare the complete workflow and a full year of expected costs.

### Should Every Employee Receive a Paid CRM Seat?

Assign seats according to required access, reporting, and editing responsibilities. Confirm each vendor's license rules before using restricted access for supporting staff.

### How Should a Company Choose Between Two CRMs?

Run the same customer workflow in both products using your intended subscription tiers. Compare task completion, reporting, export quality, and total cost.

## Choose the System Your Team Can Maintain

Start with two candidates and one shared trial checklist. Select the subscription that covers your required work at an acceptable total cost.

Before purchasing, confirm pricing, access controls, data exports, and the person responsible for administration. A manageable customer process should lead the decision when choosing CRM software for small companies.
