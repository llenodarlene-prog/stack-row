# Developer Burnout Statistics: Prevalence, Causes, and Workplace Signals

Burnout estimates vary because studies ask different questions. Some measure a clinical scale. Others ask whether someone ever felt burned out.

Job dissatisfaction, fatigue, and burnout also overlap without being identical. Good reporting keeps those measures separate.

Developer burnout appears across current surveys, but no single rate represents the profession. Stack Overflow found burnout or tech fatigue among 21.1% of respondents describing job complacency. A 2026 US IT study placed 29.1% of its analyzed sample at high burnout risk. Both samples need limitations.

## What Does Burnout Mean?

The [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/frequently-asked-questions/burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon) classifies burnout as an occupational phenomenon, rather than a medical condition.

Its ICD-11 description includes three dimensions:

1. energy depletion or exhaustion;
2. mental distance, negativity, or cynicism toward work; and
3. reduced professional effectiveness.

The definition applies to chronic workplace stress that has not been managed. It should not describe every tired or difficult week.

This boundary is important. Many developer surveys use informal wording instead of a validated burnout scale.

## What Did the Stack Overflow 2026 Survey Find?

Stack Overflow collected 30,903 valid responses from 169 countries. Seventy-five percent coded as a primary job function.

Among 18,876 respondents answering its job-satisfaction question:

- 22.2% were classified as happy;
- 32.6% were classified as unhappy; and
- 45.1% were classified as complacent.

These categories reflect the survey's scoring method. They are not clinical diagnoses.

## How Often Did Respondents Name Burnout or Tech Fatigue?

The [Stack Overflow work dataset](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/work/data) asked why some respondents felt complacent.

Among 8,312 responses, 21.1% selected burnout or tech fatigue. Economic uncertainty followed at 18.6%.

Another 9.9% cited AI automating challenging parts of the job. Lack of upward mobility reached 9.8%.

The question combined burnout with tech fatigue. It cannot provide a standalone clinical burnout prevalence rate.

## What Workplace Problems Make Developer Jobs Harder?

Stack Overflow asked 18,214 respondents which factor had made work harder during six months.

| Reported factor | Share |
|---|---:|
| Unclear priorities or requirements | 16.6% |
| Inefficient processes or coordination | 14.6% |
| Insufficient time, staffing, or resources | 14.1% |
| Leadership or organizational changes | 11.9% |
| Unreliable systems or infrastructure | 8.4% |
| Poor documentation or knowledge sharing | 7.1% |
| Weak support from colleagues or managers | 4.2% |

The top causes sit inside the work system. Personal resilience alone cannot repair unclear priorities or chronic understaffing.

## What Did the US IT Burnout Study Find?

A 2026 peer-reviewed study analyzed 292 US information-technology professionals who completed the Burnout Assessment Tool.

Eighty-five participants scored at or above the high-risk threshold. That equals 29.1% of the analyzed sample.

Forty-two participants reached the study's severe-risk threshold. That equals 14.4% of the analyzed sample.

Researchers found no meaningful burnout difference between leaders and associates. Leadership reported higher mental-health literacy.

The sample used convenience recruitment and overrepresented white male respondents. The authors warn against generalizing it across the full profession.

## What Do Global Developer Surveys Report?

The State of Devs 2026 survey reported that 62% had experienced burnout during their career. It also recorded lower motivation and more cynicism among many respondents.

A 2024 Developer Nation survey reported career burnout among 83% of nearly 1,000 participants.

These are self-selected community surveys. They ask about any point in a career, which produces higher rates than current validated screening.

The gap between 62%, 83%, and 29.1% does not prove one source is wrong. They measure different populations, periods, and definitions.

## What Does Peer-reviewed Software Research Show?

A 2024 systematic review examined 44 quantitative studies published from 2000 through 2023.

The studies covered 16,086 software professionals across at least 42 countries. Researchers found the field used varied well-being measures.

Time pressure, stress, and exhaustion were associated with errors, maintenance cost, lower performance, and turnover.

The review also found important evidence gaps. More consistent measures and stronger longitudinal studies are needed.

Association does not establish direction. Poor systems can reduce well-being, while reduced well-being can also affect work.

## Does AI Reduce or Increase Burnout?

[DORA research](https://dora.dev/ai/gen-ai-report/report/) associated a 25% increase in AI adoption with a 0.6% reduction in burnout.

The same model linked adoption to more flow and higher job satisfaction. Yet AI also associated with weaker delivery stability.

DORA describes AI as an amplifier of organizational conditions. Teams with clear systems may gain more than disorganized teams.

AI can remove some coding effort while adding review, learning, or job-security concerns. The balance differs by team.

Stack Overflow respondents often said AI was not the main cause of their work difficulties. Existing process problems remained prominent.

## What Protects Developer Well-being?

Stack Overflow asked what had made work easier during the prior six months.

Access to suitable tools led at 20.1%. Supportive colleagues, managers, or mentors followed at 17.2%.

Clear priorities helped 7.1%. Better processes helped 6.8%.

These responses support organizational changes. Teams need clarity, support, time, and dependable systems.

## How Should Managers Measure Burnout Risk?

Use anonymous, validated surveys at team level. Explain how results will be handled.

Managers can also monitor system signals:

- sustained overtime;
- repeated on-call disruptions;
- rising work-in-progress age;
- long review queues;
- frequent priority changes;
- incident load; and
- voluntary turnover.

None proves individual burnout. Together, they can reveal unhealthy work design.

Avoid surveillance. Keyboard activity, online hours, and private messages do not measure well-being.

## Which Interventions Address System Causes?

Start with the workload and workflow. Reduce unnecessary work, clarify priorities, and staff recurring operational duties.

Improve documentation and ownership. Make escalation paths visible before incidents occur.

Protect focus time and recovery after on-call events. Rotate demanding responsibilities fairly.

Train managers to recognize risk and discuss workload. Benefits programs cannot replace safer job design.

## What Should Readers Avoid Concluding?

No current source proves one burnout rate for every developer. Samples differ by country, role, recruitment, and survey wording.

An "ever experienced" question will usually exceed a current screening rate. A combined burnout-and-fatigue response is broader than ICD-11.

Company surveys may also miss people who already left. Those experiencing severe exhaustion may be less likely to participate.

Statistics should guide questions and prevention. They should not diagnose individuals.

## FAQs

### What Percentage of Developers Experience Burnout?

No single rate represents all developers. Current studies range widely because definitions, periods, samples, and screening methods differ.

### Is Burnout a Medical Condition?

The World Health Organization classifies burnout as an occupational phenomenon. It is not classified as a medical condition in ICD-11.

### What Causes Developer Burnout?

Common workplace signals include unclear priorities, inefficient processes, inadequate staffing, unreliable systems, and weak documentation.

### Can AI Reduce Burnout?

DORA found a small association between higher AI adoption and lower burnout. Team systems and work design still shape the result.

### How Should Companies Measure Burnout?

Use anonymous validated surveys and team-level operational signals. Avoid individual surveillance or productivity activity scores.
