# 7 Project Portfolio Management Software Options for Growing Teams

Project portfolio management software helps leaders choose, fund, schedule, and monitor groups of projects. It sits above daily task management. A useful platform connects strategic goals with capacity, costs, risks, and expected returns.

That distinction becomes important as project volume rises. [PMI’s 2026 Pulse report](https://www.pmi.org/about/press-media/2026/pmi-pulse-of-the-profession-report-reveals-business-acumen-as-critical-skill-to-navigate-complexity) found that roughly one-third of complex projects fail. The report also found that 97% of project professionals managed at least one complex project recently.

Project portfolio management software cannot remove complexity. It can expose weak assumptions before they become expensive commitments.

## What Separates PPM Software from Project Management Software?

Project management software helps a team deliver one project. PPM software helps leadership compare many projects competing for the same money and people.

A PPM system should answer several portfolio questions:

- Which proposals support current strategic goals?
- Which projects should start, pause, or stop?
- Where will demand exceed available capacity?
- Which investments carry the highest risk?
- What value should the active portfolio produce?
- How will a budget change affect delivery?

Basic work management can show overdue tasks. PPM adds investment selection, portfolio scenarios, resource forecasts, and executive governance.

Companies connecting projects with finance may also need an [ERP system](/business-technology/what-is-erp/). ERP records financial transactions. PPM supports decisions about proposed and active investments.

## 1. Microsoft Planner and Project Plan 3

Microsoft Planner and Project Plan 3 suits organizations already standardized on Microsoft 365. It combines premium project planning with portfolio-level views.

Plan 3 includes baselines, critical paths, advanced dependencies, and resource requests. Microsoft also supports portfolios containing premium plans. Leaders can group plans and monitor their progress together.

The [public list price](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/planner/microsoft-planner-plans-and-pricing) is $30 per user monthly with annual billing. Microsoft positions Plan 3 above Planner Plan 1 and below Plan 5.

**Best for:** Microsoft-centered organizations needing advanced schedules and portfolio visibility.

**Watch for:** Licensing can spread across project managers, contributors, and viewers. Map each role before estimating annual cost.

## 2. Planview ProjectAdvantage

Planview ProjectAdvantage targets PMOs managing strategy, demand, resources, budgets, and benefits. The product was previously known as Sciforma.

Its portfolio features cover prioritization, capacity planning, planned costs, actual costs, and expected benefits. That breadth suits organizations with formal business cases and approval gates.

Planview does not publish a simple list price. Buyers should request software, implementation, migration, and support costs separately.

**Best for:** Mature PMOs with formal investment governance and financial controls.

**Watch for:** Broad functionality can create a heavy implementation. Start with the decisions leadership needs each month.

## 3. Smartsheet Control Center

Smartsheet Control Center is a premium portfolio application built on Smartsheet. It standardizes project creation, reporting, and change management across recurring programs.

Teams can create approved blueprints for schedules, dashboards, and reports. New projects inherit that structure. Portfolio reporting then rolls information into a consistent executive view.

Control Center works especially well when projects follow repeatable patterns. Examples include store openings, customer onboarding, acquisitions, and product launches.

Pricing is quote-based and sits above the underlying Smartsheet subscription. Confirm which users need paid seats across both products.

**Best for:** Program offices launching many similar projects from reusable templates.

**Watch for:** Standardization helps only when teams follow the blueprint. Establish ownership for templates, fields, and portfolio definitions.

## 4. Celoxis

Celoxis combines project execution with portfolio, resource, risk, and financial management. It offers cloud and self-hosted deployment options.

Its [public pricing](https://www.celoxis.com/lp-project-portfolio-management-software) is unusually transparent for this category. Core starts at $10 per standard user monthly. Professional adds intake, risks, costing, and expenses at $35. Business is $45, and higher tiers use custom quotes.

Different role prices apply to team members and timesheet users. Each plan also includes a limited number of read-only users.

Celoxis can suit a PMO that needs stronger financial controls without a large enterprise contract.

**Best for:** Midmarket teams wanting portfolio controls and public per-role pricing.

**Watch for:** Features differ by tier. Compare required capabilities before using the entry price.

## 5. Meisterplan

Meisterplan focuses on portfolio and resource planning. It helps leaders test whether proposed work fits available people and strategic priorities.

Scenario planning is the central strength. A portfolio manager can compare hiring, timing, prioritization, and cancellation choices before changing the approved plan.

Meisterplan uses resource-based pricing. Its Basic plan starts at $600 monthly with annual billing. That model differs from familiar per-seat subscriptions.

The platform is most useful when resource conflicts drive portfolio decisions. It may be excessive for teams needing only shared task lists.

**Best for:** Organizations where scarce specialist capacity controls the delivery plan.

**Watch for:** Resource data becomes stale quickly. Define who maintains availability, roles, allocations, and proposed demand.

## 6. OnePlan

OnePlan provides strategic portfolio planning, scenario modeling, financial management, and resource planning. It also targets organizations with major Microsoft investments.

Portfolio views connect initiatives with objectives, costs, risks, and resource constraints. Scenario planning helps leaders compare tradeoffs before approving a roadmap.

The vendor does not publish standard prices. Request separate costs for licensing, connectors, implementation, and ongoing administration.

OnePlan deserves consideration when Microsoft tools already hold project and work data. Its value depends on integration depth and data quality.

**Best for:** Enterprises unifying portfolio decisions across Microsoft and mixed delivery tools.

**Watch for:** Integration claims need a proof of concept. Test actual fields, update direction, latency, and ownership.

## 7. Adobe Workfront

Adobe Workfront combines enterprise work management with portfolio optimization. Leaders can compare business cases using costs, risks, alignment, and expected returns.

Its Portfolio Optimizer supports project evaluation. Global resource management helps planners balance capacity across projects and teams.

Workfront also supports structured intake, approvals, and work execution. That combination suits marketing, operations, and transformation teams with complex governance.

Adobe offers three plans, Select, Prime, and Ultimate, with customized pricing. Portfolio and resource capabilities vary by plan, so buyers need a written feature map.

**Best for:** Large organizations connecting intake, execution, resources, and portfolio selection.

**Watch for:** Workfront can become a broad operating platform. Limit phase one to specific decisions and measurable workflows.

## How to Choose a PPM Platform

Start with the portfolio decision process. Software selection should follow that design.

### Define the Decision Cadence

List the decisions made weekly, monthly, and quarterly. Common examples include intake approval, reprioritization, funding changes, and capacity rebalancing.

### Separate Required Data from Attractive Dashboards

Every metric needs a source, owner, update schedule, and decision. Remove fields that nobody can maintain reliably.

### Model Total Operating Cost

Include licenses, implementation, integration, migration, training, and administration. Quote-based platforms may require substantial professional services.

### Test One Real Portfolio

Use active projects, real dependencies, and current resource constraints. A polished demonstration cannot reveal your integration or governance gaps.

### Set Adoption Measures

Track intake completeness, decision cycle time, forecast accuracy, and reporting effort. Avoid measuring success through logins alone.

PPM platforms often become another [B2B SaaS system](/saas/what-is-b2b-saas/) with recurring governance needs. Ownership should remain clear after implementation closes. Market [SaaS adoption figures](/saas/saas-statistics-revenue-adoption/) describe the category, not your portfolio fit.

## A Practical PPM Scorecard

Score each platform from one to five against weighted criteria.

| Criterion | Suggested weight | Evidence to request |
|---|---:|---|
| Portfolio prioritization | 20% | Live scoring with your business case |
| Resource capacity | 20% | Role and person-level scenario test |
| Financial management | 15% | Budget, forecast, actual, and benefit flow |
| Integration | 15% | Working connection with a production-like system |
| Reporting | 10% | Executive report built from your data |
| Governance | 10% | Intake, approval, audit, and permissions test |
| Administration | 10% | Configuration change completed by your administrator |

The weighting should reflect business constraints. A professional services firm may assign more weight to utilization and billing.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What Is the Best Project Portfolio Management Software?

The best choice matches your decision process, resource model, and financial controls. Microsoft fits its ecosystem, while dedicated PPM platforms support deeper governance.

### When Does a Company Need PPM Software?

A company needs PPM software when projects compete for shared budgets, people, or executive attention. It also helps when spreadsheet reporting consumes significant time.

### Can Project Management Software Manage a Portfolio?

Some project tools provide dashboards and portfolios. Dedicated PPM adds stronger prioritization, scenarios, financial controls, and capacity planning.

### How Much Does PPM Software Cost?

Public prices range from per-user plans to resource-based subscriptions. Enterprise vendors often require customized quotes and implementation services.
